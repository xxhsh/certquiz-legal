Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how CertQuiz handles information in relation to your use of our application. Please read this document carefully.

1. Information We Collect

CertQuiz does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The application operates entirely offline on your device.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any other personal or sensitive information

2. Information You Provide

CertQuiz does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the application. The app functions fully without registration of any kind.

3. How We Use Information

Because no personal information is collected, none is used for any purpose. All data generated by your use of the app — including your quiz answers, progress, scores, and bookmarks — is stored exclusively on your device and is never accessed by us.

4. Information Shared with Third Parties

CertQuiz does not share any information with third parties. We do not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers. No data leaves your device.

5. Data Storage

All data generated by your use of CertQuiz is stored locally on your device using Android's standard local storage mechanisms (SQLite database and SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Quiz progress and scores
  • Bookmarked questions
  • Notification preferences
  • Session history

This data remains on your device at all times. Uninstalling the application will permanently delete all stored data.

6. Security

Since CertQuiz does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of your information being intercepted or accessed by unauthorised parties through our application. Your data resides solely on your device and is subject to the security protections provided by your Android operating system.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information held by us to access, correct, or delete. You retain full control of all data generated by your use of the app:

  • You may clear your quiz progress and history at any time by uninstalling the application.
  • Notification permissions can be revoked at any time through your device's system settings.
  • No account deletion is necessary as no account is created.

8. Children's Privacy

CertQuiz does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. As no data is collected at all, the application is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version of this page and the application. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the application after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
